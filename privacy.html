Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026.
This policy applies to the mobile games we publish.

Summary

We do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. Game progress is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us. The only third party that receives any data from the app is the advertising service we use (Google AdMob).

Information processed

  • Local game data. Levels reached, in-game currency, perks, and settings are stored on your device using the operating system's standard preferences storage. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data through your device settings.
  • Advertising and device information via Google AdMob. When advertisements are displayed, AdMob may receive your advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system version, and approximate region for the purposes of delivering and measuring ads. AdMob's processing of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number.
  • Precise or coarse geographic location.
  • Access to contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • Third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Any data sent to a server we operate. We do not run a backend service for our games.

Advertising

Our games may display advertising delivered by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised based on your advertising identifier and information Google holds about you. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalisation at any time through your device's privacy settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and the per-app tracking permission prompts shown by the operating system.

Further information about how Google processes data is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Our games are intended for general audiences and are suitable for ages nine and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under thirteen, or under the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction. If you believe that a child under that age has used the app and you have concerns about their privacy, please contact us at the email address below and we will assist where appropriate.

Data retention

  • Local game data: retained on your device until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared by you.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google in accordance with its own policies.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information, we have no personal data to provide, correct, or delete on request. You may, however:

  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in your device settings.
  • Limit advertising personalisation through the operating system settings linked above.
  • Contact us with any privacy question and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of our games after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

For questions or requests relating to this policy, please email curethehollow@gmail.com.
