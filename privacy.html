Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 April 2026.
Applies to all games published by Cure the Hollow, including Draw Defender.

The short version

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or anything that identifies you. The game saves your progress to your own device using standard mobile storage and never sends that data to us. The only third party that receives any data is the ad network we use to show ads (Google AdMob), and the app stores when you make a purchase (Apple or Google).

What is collected

  • Local game progress. Levels, shards, perks, and settings are stored on your device with the operating system's standard preferences storage. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Advertising identifiers and basic device info via Google AdMob. When ads are shown, AdMob may receive your advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, and general region for the purpose of delivering and measuring ads. AdMob's use of this data is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Purchase records via the app store. If you buy an in-app product (for example, "Remove Ads"), Apple or Google handles the transaction and stores the receipt under your Apple ID / Google account. We never see your payment details. We only ask the store whether you own a product so we can unlock it for you.

What is NOT collected

  • No name, email, phone number, or account login.
  • No precise or coarse location.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (Firebase, Facebook, etc.).
  • No data sent to any server we operate. We do not operate any backend server for our games.

Advertising

Our games may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised based on your advertising identifier and the data Google has about you. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised ads at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and per-app tracking permission prompts.

For details about the data Google collects and how it is used, see Google's privacy & terms at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Our games are suitable for ages 9 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child under that age has used our app and you are concerned about their privacy, please contact us at the email below and we will assist as appropriate.

Data retention

  • Local game data: kept on your device until you delete the app or clear its data.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google per its policies.
  • Purchase records: retained by Apple or Google per their policies.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personally identifying information, we have no personal data to delete or hand over. You can still:

  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.
  • Limit ad tracking using the OS settings linked above.
  • Contact us with any privacy question and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our games after a change means you accept the updated policy.
